New thing
Today: lots on one screen, your colours and icons. Six tabs, and many buttons of its own kind (pills, steps, chips), so it feels less like an iPhone app.
A: calm and familiar. But icons in coloured squares, grey-blue everywhere and taller rows mean less fits on a screen, and your colours and drawn icons are gone.
B (my pick): Apple's order (big titles, back arrow, grouped lists, floating tab bar, 5 tabs) with your colours, drawn icons, tight rows and a full-colour main button.
The same in A and B:
  • Five tabs instead of six (Apple's limit on iPhone). Settings moves to the nut at the top right of Home.
  • The calendar for a new trip moves off Home into its own page behind "New trip".
  • Every page gets a big title and a back arrow, and buttons sit in a floating bar at the bottom.
  • On the Mac: a sidebar on the left instead of tabs, and the normal window title bar comes back.
These are drawings, not the finished app. Exact sizes come when it is built, and nothing you placed moves without your yes.
